# Privacy

Rival Signal does not implement telemetry, advertising, user accounts, tracking pixels, or outbound data uploads. Files are processed by the running Streamlit application. If someone deploys the app, that operator controls infrastructure logs, retention, authentication, backups, and network access and must document those practices separately.

Do not upload personal or confidential data to a deployment you do not control. Prefer de-identified identifiers and apply appropriate access, retention, and disclosure controls.

Rival Signal makes no AI or web requests. The research prompt is text you copy into an assistant of your choice; whatever you paste there, including your brief and any source material, goes to that provider under its terms. Source links in a project are shown as links only and are never fetched by the app.

Inside Signal Hub, Rival Signal runs in Hub mode: uploads stay in the session's memory, the fictional demo is preloaded, and nothing is written to disk. Download the project JSON to keep your work.
